college_grad_df %>% drop_na(sharewomen, median) %>%

## `geom_smooth()` using formula = 'y ~ x'

coef(lm(median ~ sharewomen, data = college_grad_df))
## (Intercept)  sharewomen 
##    56093.31   -30669.94

From the scatter plot, there is a distinct negative correlation between a higher share of women and majors with lower median earnings, the formula of the line of best fit being: y = 56100 - 30700x, where y is median and x is sharewomen. Additionally, the negative gradient of -30700 indicates that for every 1 unit increase in the proportion of women, the predicted median earnings decrease by roughly $30,700.
